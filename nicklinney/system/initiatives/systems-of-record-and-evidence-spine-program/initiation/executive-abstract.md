## Executive Summary

The CIO retrospective shows that the enterprise can catalog and document more effectively than it can yet prove system and data operations. This initiative responds by establishing a bounded systems-of-record and evidence-spine program so that authoritative records, ownership, and evidence-retention rules can be defined before broader system-realization claims are advanced.

## Initiative Purpose

The purpose of the initiative is to create a clear and governable systems-of-record model for the enterprise by defining:

- authoritative record locations for system, data, and governance artifacts
- ownership and stewardship responsibilities for those record sets
- evidence-retention and completeness expectations
- recurring review packs that connect systems, data, and documentation evidence

## Why This Initiative Now

The closed ECI corpus found `NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement` to be the strongest practical foothold in the CIO portfolio, while `NickLinney.System` and `NickLinney.DataInfrastructure` remain under-realized. That makes this an appropriate sequencing move: strengthen record authority and evidence traceability now so later system and data initiatives can be assessed against a clearer factual base.

## Initial Deliverables

- executive abstract
- systems-of-record map for system, data, and governance artifacts
- ownership model for authoritative records
- evidence-retention and completeness review standards

## Initial Success Condition

The initiative will have succeeded at this first stage when the enterprise can trace major system, data, and governance claims back to explicit authoritative records with named ownership and repeatable evidence-retention rules.
