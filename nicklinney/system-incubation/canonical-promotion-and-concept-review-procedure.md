# Canonical Promotion and Concept Review Procedure

## Status

- Status: Canonical
- Canonicality: Canonical
- Origin: Wiki-native procedure artifact
- Created: July 27, 2026
- Owning Space: `System`

## Purpose

This procedure defines how incubating concepts within `System` are reviewed and, where appropriate, promoted into canonical ecosystem modules.

## Procedure

1. Identify the concept and its intended architectural domain.
2. Record the concept as a distinct reviewable object.
3. Evaluate maturity, scope clarity, and governance impact.
4. Determine whether the concept belongs in `System`, `NickLinney.System`, or another canonical module.
5. If approved for promotion, publish the canonical artifact in the target module.
6. Record the promotion relationship and residual incubation notes.

## Minimum Review Questions

- Is the concept still exploratory or already authoritative in practice?
- Does it duplicate an existing canonical artifact?
- What module should own it once promoted?
- What governance or dependency impact does promotion create?
