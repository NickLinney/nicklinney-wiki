## Status

## Purpose

This taxonomy defines the thematic clusters used to classify incubating research concepts within `System`.

## Research Clusters

- ontology
- epistemology and knowledge representation
- graph and relationship architecture
- cybernetics and systems theory
- orchestration and control models
- governance and promotion logic

## Governance Rule

Every incubating concept should be classifiable into at least one primary research cluster to support review and discoverability.
