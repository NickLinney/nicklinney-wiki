- Status: Canonical

## Purpose

This procedure governs the lifecycle of incubation experiments from initial declaration through review, supersession, archival, or promotion.

## Lifecycle Stages

1. Declare the experiment or concept object.
2. Assign research cluster and intended question.
3. Record review checkpoints and evidence expectations.
4. Classify outcome as continue, supersede, archive, or promote.
5. Preserve lineage to downstream canonical artifacts where applicable.

## Governance Rule

Incubation work should remain historically legible even when experiments fail, merge, or are superseded.
