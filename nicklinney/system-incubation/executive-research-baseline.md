# System Executive Research Baseline

## Status

- Status: Canonical
- Canonicality: Canonical
- Origin: Wiki-native baseline artifact
- Created: July 27, 2026

## Executive Summary

`System` is the research and incubation space in which conceptual structures are explored before they are promoted into canonical ecosystem modules.

It exists to provide a disciplined place for experimentation in ontology, information theory, graph architecture, cybernetics, and systems design without prematurely treating those ideas as authoritative portfolio doctrine.

## Strategic Role

The role of `System` is to protect architectural maturity.

It allows exploratory work to accumulate, evolve, and be challenged before it becomes part of the governed canonical substrate represented by `NickLinney.System` or other mature modules.

## Why It Exists

Without a distinct incubation layer, experimental concepts can be mistaken for authoritative architecture too early.

`System` provides the buffer that preserves both innovation and governance by separating research from canon.

## Minimum Future Artifact Set

This space should eventually gain:

- research charter
- promotion criteria into canonical modules
- experiment index
- concept review records
