## Executive Summary

`NickLinney.AutoGit` is the source-control governance and automation discipline of the ecosystem.

It exists to define how repositories are created, maintained, versioned, attributed, and released under governed Git workflows, including future machine-assisted contribution models.

## Strategic Role

This module protects the integrity of the software estate by turning Git operations into a managed governance surface rather than a collection of local habits.

## Why It Exists

As automation and agentic contribution expand, repository hygiene, authorship, branching discipline, and release control become strategic concerns. `NickLinney.AutoGit` exists to provide that control layer.
