# NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement

## Purpose

NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement governs how the NickLinney.* ecosystem captures, structures, versions, recovers, publishes, supersedes, and preserves knowledge artifacts over time.

Its core mission is to ensure that documentation is not merely written, but remains:

- authoritative
- discoverable
- versioned
- historically retained
- provenance-aware
- recoverable from fragmented origins
- classifiable across governance, strategy, projects, products, operations, and reference material

## Problem Statement

The enterprise inventory shows that the primary knowledge problem is not absence of documentation, but fragmentation of document state.

The dominant failure modes are:

- canonical artifacts not existing as durable, individually controlled records
- chat-generated markdown remaining embedded inside conversations
- standards and procedures existing only as sections within larger documents
- incomplete declaration of ownership, approval authority, version, supersession, and canonical source

## Goal

Populate and maintain the wiki with full versioning and historical retention while preserving authority, provenance, and cross-document relationships.

## Governing Concerns

This module is responsible for:

- document control and publication states
- document naming and ownership conventions
- enterprise document register structure
- recovery of chat-generated artifacts into controlled records
- declaration of canonical source when copies diverge
- version-history expectations for wiki publication and supersession
