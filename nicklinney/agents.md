# NickLinney.Agents

## Purpose

`NickLinney.Agents` defines the governed population of digital workers that operate within the NickLinney.* ecosystem.

Where `SyntheOS` and broader systems architecture define how artificial agents think and execute, `NickLinney.Agents` defines who those agents are, what responsibilities they hold, how they are governed, and how their identities evolve over time.

## Module Role

This module exists to preserve and govern:

- agent identities
- role and department definitions
- behavioral specifications
- lifecycle state and succession
- compatibility with platform and governance standards
- institutional continuity for digital workers

## Architectural Boundary

`NickLinney.Agents` should be understood as distinct from adjacent but related concerns:

- `SyntheOS` is the cognitive operating system and execution substrate.
- `NickLinney.Agents` is the governed catalog of digital personnel and identities.
- `NickLinney.SoftwareFactory` is the enterprise and production methodology within which those workers operate.
