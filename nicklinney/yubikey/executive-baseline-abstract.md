## Executive Summary

`NickLinney.YubiKey` is the hardware-rooted identity and trust module of the ecosystem.

It exists to govern how physical trust anchors are used for authentication, authorization, signing, secrets protection, and recovery-sensitive identity workflows.

## Strategic Role

This module provides a concrete operational trust boundary for high-assurance identity and access practices across the portfolio.

## Why It Exists

When identity is purely software-defined, recovery, attribution, and compromise boundaries weaken. `NickLinney.YubiKey` exists to strengthen those boundaries through hardware-backed trust.
