## Purpose

This artifact defines the precedence order for decisions involving hardware-backed trust and identity controls.

## Policy Precedence

1. Constitutional safety and security governance.
2. Hardware trust procedures and issuance policy.
3. Recovery and replacement controls.
4. Use-case-specific authorization decisions.

## Governance Rule

Hardware trust should be managed under explicit precedence rules so that convenience decisions do not silently override assurance posture.
