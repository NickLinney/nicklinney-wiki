## Purpose

This procedure defines the minimum controlled response flow for operational security incidents.

## Procedure

1. Detect and classify the event.
2. Confirm ownership and escalation path.
3. Preserve relevant evidence.
4. Contain or isolate the affected surface as appropriate.
5. Record risk posture and response decisions.
6. Coordinate remediation and recovery.
7. Publish the post-incident record or downstream governance artifact where required.

## Minimum Incident Record Elements

- incident identifier
- detection source
- severity level
- accountable owner
- containment action
- recovery status
- follow-up requirements
