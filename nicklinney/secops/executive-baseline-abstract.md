## Executive Summary

`NickLinney.SecOps` is the operational cybersecurity discipline of the ecosystem.

It exists to govern monitoring, detection, incident response, and defensive automation as continuous security operations rather than isolated control activities.

## Strategic Role

This module provides the operational feedback loop that allows security governance to remain active, measurable, and responsive in production conditions.

## Why It Exists

Security policy without operational observation is incomplete. `NickLinney.SecOps` exists to turn security posture into ongoing defensive practice.
