## Executive Summary

The CFO retrospective identifies contract governance as an enterprise control gap: doctrine exists, but there is not yet runtime proof that obligations, interfaces, and compatibility expectations are being governed in a recurring way. This initiative responds with a bounded contract-lifecycle control program aimed at making contractual control a reviewable operating capability rather than a design aspiration.

## Initiative Purpose

The purpose of the initiative is to establish a canonical contract lifecycle and evidence model that can govern:

- contract standards for internal interfaces and governed obligations
- version, compatibility, and change-control records
- review obligations across development, release, and operational handoff
- evidence expectations showing that contract requirements are actually used

## Why This Initiative Now

The closed ECI corpus found `NickLinney.Contracts` to be conceptually respectable but under-proven as a live governance capability. That creates avoidable ambiguity around interface expectations and obligations. A bounded contract-lifecycle program is therefore an evidence-led step that improves control without implying that broad contract adoption has already occurred.

## Initial Deliverables

- executive abstract
- canonical contract standard and lifecycle model
- version and compatibility record expectations
- review and evidence obligations for governed contract use

## Initial Success Condition

The initiative will have succeeded at this first stage when the enterprise has a reviewable contract-lifecycle standard with evidence requirements strong enough to support one governed interface or obligation pathway.
