## Executive Summary

`NickLinney.Contracts` is the interoperability discipline of the ecosystem.

It exists to define the formal agreements that allow modules, services, agents, and systems to exchange information and behavior predictably across time.

## Strategic Role

This module protects architectural coherence by ensuring that integration is governed by explicit contracts rather than informal assumptions.

## Why It Exists

As the ecosystem grows, stable boundaries become more important than ad hoc compatibility. `NickLinney.Contracts` provides the structure required for versioned, governable, and auditable interoperation.
