# System Incubation Project

## Status

- Status: Canonical
- Canonicality: Canonical
- Origin: Wiki-native baseline artifact
- Created: July 27, 2026
- Updated: July 27, 2026 with standards-layer and third-layer artifacts

## Purpose

`System` is the distinct research and incubation space used to explore concepts before they are promoted into canonical `NickLinney.*` modules.

It is not the same object as `NickLinney.System`.

Where `NickLinney.System` represents the authoritative semantic and architectural substrate of the ecosystem, `System` represents pre-canonical exploration, experimentation, and conceptual development.

## Scope

This space is intended to hold:

- ontology research
- information theory exploration
- graph and relationship modeling experiments
- cybernetic and systems-theory incubations
- pre-canonical architectural ideas
- conceptual records that may later mature into governed module artifacts

## Boundary

- `System` is experimental and pre-canonical.
- `NickLinney.System` is canonical and authoritative once ideas are promoted.
- Promotion from `System` into canonical modules should occur through explicit review and formal artifact publication.
