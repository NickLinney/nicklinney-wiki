## Executive Summary

`NickLinney.DevOps` is the automation and delivery discipline of the ecosystem.

It exists to ensure that build, validation, infrastructure, deployment, and release workflows operate as governed systems rather than disconnected tooling practices.

## Strategic Role

This module turns delivery capability into a repeatable operational asset that scales with the rest of the portfolio.

## Why It Exists

Without a governed DevOps layer, engineering speed tends to increase variability instead of reliability. `NickLinney.DevOps` exists to make automation trustworthy, inspectable, and operationally sustainable.
