## Decision

NickLinney.Ops records the decision to create and maintain `Applying the ECI Process to the Enterprise` as a standing operating guidance document within the ECEGF suite.

## Context

The 2026 enterprise investigation proved that the ECI process can be executed across a complex, multi-module enterprise and can recursively generate module assessments, enterprise synthesis, cabinet interpretation, publication controls, and executive retrospective follow-on work. That execution also revealed a documentation gap: the enterprise now possesses a practiced application pattern, but not a concise reusable document explaining how to repeat that pattern for future ECI targets.

The Chief Vision Officer identified the need for a reusable process-application document. The Chief Information Officer tasked the Director of Knowledge Management with drafting it.

## Rationale

- The enterprise now has enough lived practice to document the ECI process as an operating pattern rather than only as a conceptual framework.
- Future users should not need to reconstruct the process from the entire investigation corpus.
- A reusable process-application guide improves consistency across future assessments of modules, enterprises, projects, incidents, departments, and other eligible ECI targets.
- Recording the document as an ADR-backed addition makes the guidance governable without retroactively modifying the completed 2026 event series.

## Scope

This ADR authorizes:

- creation of one new operating guidance document, `Applying the ECI Process to the Enterprise`
- treatment of that document as forward-looking support material for future ECI executions
- use of the 2026 enterprise investigation as the modeled reference implementation for the guide

This ADR does not authorize:

- retroactive alteration of historical investigation artifacts
- automatic reclassification of prior assessments
- expansion of the completed 2026 ECI scope

## Consequences

- Future ECI operators have a concise guide for applying the framework to new targets.
- The ECEGF suite gains a practical bridge between framework theory and enterprise execution.
- The new guide can be revised later if future ECI cycles materially change the process or add target-specific patterns.
