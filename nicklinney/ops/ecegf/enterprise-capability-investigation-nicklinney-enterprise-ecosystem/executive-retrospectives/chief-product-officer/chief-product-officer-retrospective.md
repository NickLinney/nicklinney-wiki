## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The CPO portfolio governs software assets, development discipline, and factory-style product creation. The question is whether the enterprise can prove one governed software-delivery pathway end to end.

## Reading of the Investigation

This is one of the sharpest enterprise gaps: rich design and operating-model intent without comparable runtime evidence.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.Software` lacks a proven live software-asset registry. `NickLinney.SoftwareDevelopment` has visible standards but not active governed development evidence. `NickLinney.SoftwareFactory` is richly described, yet not proven as a deployed runtime capability.

## Cross-Module Observations

These modules should behave as one product-delivery chain, but the investigation warns against assuming they already do. The portfolio needs one delivery case that touches all three.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish a `Governed Software Delivery Pilot Program`

## Action Plan

The objective is to execute one full governed software delivery case through a controlled asset register, development process, and factory-style reporting discipline. Success evidence would include software register entries, development completion evidence, and linked runtime reporting records.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

Next year's ECI should be able to assess one real software delivery pathway rather than three largely separate design bodies.
