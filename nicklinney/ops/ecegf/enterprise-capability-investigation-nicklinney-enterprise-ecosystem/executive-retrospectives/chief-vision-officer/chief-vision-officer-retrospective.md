## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The CVO is responsible for strategic direction and module-system coherence, and therefore for whether the enterprise can truthfully describe both its intended direction and its internal structure.

## Reading of the Investigation

The investigation validated the ECI process as a practical executive instrument for the enterprise's current maturity phase, while also showing that the enterprise is strongly designed and still under-realized.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.Strategy` and `NickLinney.Modules` both remain `In Progress / Not Realized` at RCMF `1 / 5`, despite materially stronger design positions. The critical gap is not lack of thought, but lack of governed runtime proof.

## Cross-Module Observations

The CVO reads the most consequential gap as the absence of a sufficiently governed identity-and-access spine capable of binding ownership, permissions, secret distribution, and cross-agent trust into one reviewable model.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish the `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` Strategy and Implementation Project

## Action Plan

The objective is to define and begin implementing a formal identity-and-access capability including RBAC, secrets access governance, vault strategy, and multi-agent trust-boundary control. Success evidence would include module definition, governance charter, approved role maps, and recurring review artifacts.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

A successful identity-and-access initiative should improve the truthfulness, coherence, and governability of the enterprise core and sharpen future ECI findings.
