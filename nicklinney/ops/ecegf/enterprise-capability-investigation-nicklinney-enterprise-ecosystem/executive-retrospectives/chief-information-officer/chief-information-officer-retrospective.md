## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The CIO evaluates system logic, information flows, and evidence traceability through the question of what is authoritative and how it can be proven.

## Reading of the Investigation

The enterprise can document and catalog more effectively than it can yet operationalize systems and data services. That asymmetry is useful because it provides an evidence spine for more mature information governance.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.System` and `NickLinney.DataInfrastructure` remain not realized. `NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement` is realized in narrow scope and is the strongest practical anchor in the portfolio.

## Cross-Module Observations

Without system-of-record clarity, both system and data claims remain vulnerable to ambiguity. Without knowledge-management discipline, even improved system and data work becomes harder to prove.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish a `Systems of Record and Evidence Spine Program`

## Action Plan

The objective is to define authoritative record locations, ownership, and evidence-retention rules for system, data, and governance artifacts. Success evidence would include a systems-of-record map, data-governance ownership, evidence-retention checks, and recurring linked review packs.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

Next year's ECI should be able to trace major claims back to clear systems of record with less ambiguity and better reassessment reliability.
