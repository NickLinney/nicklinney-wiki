## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The CEO reads the investigation through `NickLinney.BusinessDesign`, because this module defines whether the enterprise can translate design intelligence into a governed management system.

## Reading of the Investigation

The enterprise does not lack patterns or strategic structure. It lacks strong runtime proof that those patterns are governing live work.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.BusinessDesign` holds SICAF `4 / 5` and re-baselined RCMF `1 / 5`. Its outputs are abundant, but they are primarily evidenced as design artifacts rather than as a recurring operating capability.

## Cross-Module Observations

Weak operating proof in business design amplifies risk across downstream governance because other modules inherit conceptual logic from it without equivalent runtime evidence.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish a `Business Design Operating Standards and Governance Program`

## Action Plan

The objective is to convert high-value business-design patterns into controlled operating standards with named ownership, recurring review, and evidence-bearing outputs. Success evidence would include approved standards, recurring governance records, and traceable use of those standards in live work.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

Next year's ECI should be able to inspect business design as a live control object rather than only as conceptual source material.
