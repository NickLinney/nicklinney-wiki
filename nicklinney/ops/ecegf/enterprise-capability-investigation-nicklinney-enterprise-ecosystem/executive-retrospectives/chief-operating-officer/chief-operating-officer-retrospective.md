## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The COO evaluates whether the enterprise has enough operational proof to support routine execution rather than merely strategic intent.

## Reading of the Investigation

The ECI shows real operating footholds in `NickLinney.Ops` and `NickLinney.env`, but insufficient proof of broad runtime service maturity, especially in hosting.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.Ops` and `NickLinney.env` are realized in narrow scope. `NickLinney.InfrastructureHosting` remains not realized because procedure exists without proof of active hosted implementation.

## Cross-Module Observations

These modules form one operating chain: cadence, environment discipline, and runtime hosting. The chain is credible as a governance base, but incomplete as a reliability base.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish an `Operations Service Catalog and Runtime Evidence Program`

## Action Plan

The objective is to define service ownership, support expectations, runtime measures, and evidence requirements across operations, environment, and hosting. Success evidence would include a service catalog, support standards, uptime and deployment records, and recurring KPI/KRI review packs.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

Next year's ECI should be able to assess a cleaner operational control plane with more honest runtime reliability claims.
