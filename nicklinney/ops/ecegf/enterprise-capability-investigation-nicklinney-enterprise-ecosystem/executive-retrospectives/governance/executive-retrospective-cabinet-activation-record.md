## Activation Basis

This record activates the executive retrospective phase that follows the `Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair`. The activation is governed as a child work package of the same `Enterprise Capability Investigation: NickLinney.Enterprise Ecosystem` operational event.

The activating guidance from the Chief Vision Officer is treated as controlling for this phase:

- the investigation has demonstrated strong diligence, low strategic drift, and high governance observability
- the next useful step is not to resolve every gap at once, but for each executive to conduct a private thought exercise and produce an evidence-led retrospective
- each executive retrospective must identify one top action item for the upcoming year in the form of a project, product, improvement, feature, framework, or standard intended to close meaningful capability gaps and raise future RCMF scores

## Scope

This activation includes:

- constitution of the full executive cabinet as retrospective authors
- assignment of primary module responsibility across the assessed module set
- authoring guidance for executive retrospective artifacts
- tracking of retrospective outputs and top action-item proposals

This activation excludes:

- immediate execution of all proposed interventions
- modification of completed module or enterprise assessment scores unless a later governed reassessment is opened
- premature incorporation of future modules into the completed 2026 investigation baseline

## Governing Rules

1. Each executive retrospective must reason from the existing `RCMF vs. SICAF Capability Gap Report and Action Plan` artifacts for the modules assigned to that executive.
2. The realization re-baseline remains controlling. Unsupported claims remain `In Progress / Not Realized`.
3. Each executive must propose only one top action item for the coming year.
4. Proposed future work should remain bounded, evidence-led, and proportionate to the gaps identified.
5. `NickLinney.Strategy` and `NickLinney.Modules` remain under primary responsibility of the Chief Vision Officer, with the Director of Knowledge Management serving as the designated coherence partner for `NickLinney.Modules`.

## Declared CVO Proposal Seed

The Chief Vision Officer has already declared a leading proposal seed for the coming retrospective cycle:

- establish an `Identity and Access Management Strategy and Implementation Project`
- create a new capability module named `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement`
- include a comprehensive RBAC schema, direct implementation plan, RBAC secrets access model, and secure vault access strategy for federated distribution of secrets in multi-agent architectures

This proposal seed is recorded here as a declared candidate action item. It does not alter the completed 2026 ECI scope or module register unless separately governed in a future project.
