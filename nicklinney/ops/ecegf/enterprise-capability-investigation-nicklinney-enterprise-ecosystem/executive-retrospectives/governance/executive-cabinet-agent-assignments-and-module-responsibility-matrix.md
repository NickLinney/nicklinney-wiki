# Executive Cabinet Agent Assignments and Module Responsibility Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix assigns executive agents to the executive cabinet for retrospective authorship and distributes primary module responsibility across the assessed enterprise according to role proximity, existing body of work, and enterprise remit.

## Executive Agent Roster

| Executive Role | Assigned Agent | Primary Module Responsibility | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Operating Officer (COO) | Executive Agent `COO-01` | `NickLinney.Ops`; `NickLinney.env`; `NickLinney.InfrastructureHosting` | These modules define operating cadence, environment discipline, and day-to-day delivery reliability. |
| Chief Vision Officer (CVO) | Executive Agent `CVO-01` | `NickLinney.BusinessDesign`; `NickLinney.Strategy`; `NickLinney.Modules` | Enterprise design, strategy ownership, and module-system coherence are reserved to the CVO, with ongoing coordination with Knowledge Management. |
| Chief Information Officer (CIO) | Executive Agent `CIO-01` | `NickLinney.System`; `NickLinney.DataInfrastructure`; `NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement` | These modules govern enterprise information flows, systems-of-record logic, and evidence traceability. |
| Chief Technology Officer (CTO) | Executive Agent `CTO-01` | `NickLinney.Agents`; `NickLinney.AutoGit`; `NickLinney.DevOps`; `SyntheOS` | These modules form the automation, platform, and technical enablement spine of the enterprise. |
| Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | Executive Agent `CFO-01` | `NickLinney.Contracts` | Contractual control, exposure management, and enterprise obligations align most directly to the CFO remit. |

## Cross-Cabinet Coordination Rules

- The CVO retains primary authority over `NickLinney.BusinessDesign`, `NickLinney.Strategy`, and `NickLinney.Modules`.
- The rest of the cabinet should respond to CVO or Knowledge Management inquiries concerning `NickLinney.Modules` as needed, but should not claim primary authorship over that module.
- Executives may cite cross-module dependencies in their retrospectives, but primary reasoning and action planning should remain anchored to their assigned modules.
- Where a proposed action item materially affects another executive's module set, that dependency should be identified explicitly rather than silently absorbed.

## Assignment Notes

- No equal-load requirement is imposed. The assignment principle is role proximity, not numerical symmetry.
- The executive retrospectives are intended to generate differentiated judgment. Artificially flattening module ownership would reduce signal quality.
- `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` is recorded as a forward-looking CVO proposal seed and is not part of the assessed 2026 module set.
- No CEO role is used in this structure. Enterprise design responsibility remains under the Chief Vision Officer.
