## Executive Perspective and Role Basis

The CMO is accountable for communications coherence, channel stewardship, and message governance. This retrospective evaluates whether `NickLinney.Comms` is an active capability or still a conceptual frontier.

## Reading of the Investigation

Communications capability has been explored conceptually, but not yet stabilized as a governed operating function. That boundedness makes it suitable for a controlled pilot.

## Module Reasoning

`NickLinney.Comms` lacks direct evidence of active deployment. The immediate need is not sophistication but one controlled internal workflow with security and service expectations made explicit.

## Cross-Module Observations

Communications depends on operations, security, and documentation because any real workflow will require service rules, access boundaries, and retained records.

## Top Action Item Proposal

- establish an `Internal Communications Workflow Pilot Program`

## Action Plan

The objective is to pilot one internal communications workflow with documented security, review, and evidence expectations. Success evidence would include a scope note, service requirements, pilot records, and review logs.

## Expected Gap-Closure Effect on Next Year's ECI

Next year's ECI should be able to assess at least one live communications workflow as a governed capability rather than a conceptual possibility.
