## Purpose

This activity records the controlled ingestion of the investigation corpus into the canonical local WikiJS knowledge estate. It exists to ensure that all generated investigation documents are admitted into the wiki as durable navigable objects, and that the `Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair` points to working wiki-native references wherever the target exists inside the investigation corpus.

## Scope

This activity includes:

- publication of investigation event artifacts into WikiJS
- publication of module assessment packages into WikiJS
- publication of enterprise synthesis and postmortem cabinet artifacts into WikiJS
- repair of broken filesystem backlinks on the Executive Chair report page
- verification that cited investigation documents exist at working wiki paths

This activity excludes:

- reinterpretation of investigation findings
- material revision of previously approved report language except where needed to repair link targets
- admission of external transcript sources as if they were canonical wiki-native records

## Governing Rule

Internal investigation references should resolve to wiki-native pages when the target document is part of the investigation corpus. External transcript or retrieval-corpus evidence may remain external where no canonical wiki-native counterpart is intended.

## Planned Outputs

- published wiki path set for the investigation corpus
- corrected Executive Chair report links
- updated artifact register reflecting the publication activity

## Completion Standard

This activity is complete when the investigation corpus generated in the canonical project has been published into WikiJS under a stable namespace and the Executive Chair report no longer relies on broken filesystem links for internal investigation references.
