# NickLinney.SoftwareDevelopment - Action Plan

## Planning Position

This plan converts the module assessment into a practical improvement sequence. It assumes that the next best step is to strengthen evidence and accountability before attempting broad optimization.

## Priority Workstreams

| Workstream | Objective | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance baseline | Clarify ownership, boundaries, and decision rights | Better accountability and lower ambiguity |
| Evidence discipline | Define the minimum records that prove the capability is operating | Stronger auditability and more reliable scoring |
| Operating cadence | Introduce recurring reviews and management reporting | Earlier issue detection and better control reliability |

## 90-Day Plan

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Success measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-30 days | Convert guides and implied standards into controlled templates and procedures | Capability owner | Scope, ownership, and governance baseline are published |
| 0-30 days | Define minimum evidence requirements for this module | Governance Lead | Evidence standard is documented |
| 31-60 days | Establish completion evidence, testing evidence, and release-readiness criteria | Operations Lead | First module evidence pack is available |
| 31-90 days | Run one full governed development case through the module | Capability owner | Review cadence is operating with logged outputs |

## Approval Logic

Actions should be prioritized because they reduce the difference between what the enterprise intends this module to do and what the current evidence shows it can reliably sustain.
