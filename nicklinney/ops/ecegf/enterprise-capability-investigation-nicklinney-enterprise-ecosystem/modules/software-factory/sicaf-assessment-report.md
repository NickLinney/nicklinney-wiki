## Evidence Statement

The SICAF result is anchored in current documentary evidence and adjacent enterprise references. Confidence is high because the corpus is design-heavy and does not always contain formalized architecture-control artifacts at the level expected of a mature governed enterprise capability.

## Strategic Risks and Dependencies

| Risk or dependency | Why it matters | Suggested response |

## Recommended Actions
