# Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair - Draft Proposal Skeleton

## Planned Sections

1. Cabinet mandate and evidentiary standard
2. Enterprise condition overview
3. High-impact architecture and dependency findings
4. High-impact security and assurance findings
5. High-impact operations and reporting findings
6. High-impact documentation and registry findings
7. High-impact portfolio and delivery findings
8. Significant gaps
9. Outstanding opportunities
10. Recommended paths of gap amelioration
11. Recommended paths of strategic advancement
12. Director-led cabinet recommendations to the Executive Chair

## Drafting Rule

The draft proposal must:

- preserve the realization re-baseline as the controlling evidentiary standard
- avoid personal interpretation by the executives
- rely on director evidence and module or enterprise backlinks
- distinguish realized operational footing from conceptual or design maturity
