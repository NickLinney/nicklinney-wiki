## Constitutional Purpose

This cabinet exists to assemble an evidence-based postmortem review of the `Enterprise Capability Investigation: NickLinney.Enterprise Ecosystem` and to deliver an `Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair` that identifies:

- specific high-impact items
- significant gaps
- outstanding opportunities
- recommended paths of gap amelioration
- recommended paths of strategic advancement

The cabinet will not render final interpretive judgment on behalf of the Chief Vision Officer. Its role is to prepare a director-led evidence package that the Executive Chair and Chief Vision Officer may investigate further to their own satisfaction.

## Executive Cabinet Composition

The cabinet is formed at the executive level without the Chief Vision Officer as an active participant in this phase.

| Role | Assignment | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presiding Chair | Chief Operations Officer (COO) | Established executive-chair precedent in Business Design cabinet materials |
| Executive Oversight Member | Chief Information Officer / Acting Chief Information Security Officer | Technical governance and assurance perspective |
| Executive Oversight Member | Chief Financial Officer | Margin, risk, and prioritization discipline |
| Recording Secretary | Corporate Governance Liaison | Established cabinet-recording precedent |

## Director Corps Appointed to the Cabinet

The executives appoint the following director team to prepare the working report:

| Director Role | Mandate |
| --- | --- |
| Director of Enterprise Architecture and Technical Governance | Surface systemic architecture gaps, dependency risks, and governance-boundary concerns |
| Director of Security Governance and Assurance | Surface security, assurance, and evidence-discipline findings |
| Director of Operations Governance | Surface operating cadence, reporting, and runtime-governance findings |
| Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance | Surface evidence quality, documentation integrity, traceability, and historiographic findings |
| Director of Portfolio and Delivery Governance | Surface delivery, sequencing, and cross-module execution findings |

## Chairing Rule

The COO serves as chair and must maintain order, preserve scope, recognize motions, and break ties when required, without imposing personal interpretation.

## Meeting Procedure

The cabinet follows the required cadence for each phase:

1. Director agents collect evidence and prepare presentations for the current phase.
2. Cabinet meeting is held according to Robert's Rules of Order.

### Additional Procedural Constraints

- Commentary is limited to official motions and action items.
- Cabinet members are allowed one comment action per item under discussion.
- Comment threads may not have child comment threads.
- The chair breaks ties.

## Required Phases

| Phase | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Initial notes and observations |
| Phase 2 | Draft proposal |
| Phase 3 | Final report |

## Deliverable Standard

The final report must be stored in the wiki as an artifact with local-file backlinks to the underlying investigation evidence so that the Executive Chair, Chief Vision Officer, or other reviewing authority can trace claims back to the source corpus.
