# Cabinet Operating Note

## Reporting Rule

Director work in this cabinet is evidence-led and phase-bound. Directors should not contribute broad executive interpretation or speculative strategic philosophy. They should contribute only findings, motions, and action-oriented recommendations grounded in the evidence corpus.

## Report Destination

The culminating artifact for this cabinet is:

- `Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair`

## Evidence Reference Standard

Every material claim in the final report should point back to one or more local artifacts, using clickable wiki-local backlinks where possible. Preferred sources include:

- the enterprise synthesis artifacts
- the module-level `RCMF vs. SICAF Capability Gap Report and Action Plan` artifacts
- the realization re-baseline register
- the relevant Business Design governance materials that justify the cabinet structure
