## Official Motions and Actions

### Item 1 - Adoption of Controlling Evidentiary Standard

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet adopt the realization re-baseline as the controlling evidentiary standard for all cabinet findings, notes, draft work, and final reporting, and that unsupported capability claims be excluded or explicitly marked In Progress / Not Realized.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Enterprise Architecture and Technical Governance: supported the motion on dependency-honesty grounds, citing the high-gap, non-realized status of `NickLinney.System`, `SyntheOS`, `NickLinney.Modules`, and `NickLinney.DataInfrastructure`.
- Director of Security Governance and Assurance: supported the motion on assurance grounds, citing the need to prevent false confidence in `NickLinney.Security`, `NickLinney.SecOps`, and `NickLinney.YubiKey`.
- Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance: supported the motion on provenance grounds, citing uncertainty around durable document existence in the inventory corpus.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- Recording Secretary will treat [Realization Rebaseline Register](/nicklinney/ops/ecegf/enterprise-capability-investigation-nicklinney-enterprise-ecosystem/event/tracking/realization-rebaseline-register) as the controlling cabinet baseline.

### Item 2 - Priority Subject Classification for Phase 2

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet classify the following as priority Phase 2 draft subjects: architecture-critical realization gaps, security realization gaps, documentation-governance dependencies, and delivery-governance realization gaps.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Enterprise Architecture and Technical Governance: identified `NickLinney.System`, `SyntheOS`, `NickLinney.Modules`, and `NickLinney.DataInfrastructure` as architecture-critical.
- Director of Security Governance and Assurance: identified `NickLinney.Security`, `NickLinney.SecOps`, and `NickLinney.YubiKey` as security-critical.
- Director of Portfolio and Delivery Governance: identified `NickLinney.Strategy`, `NickLinney.SoftwareFactory`, `NickLinney.SoftwareDevelopment`, and `NickLinney.DevOps` as delivery-critical.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- Phase 2 draft work will be organized around the four priority subject groups named in the motion.

### Item 3 - Evidence Spine for Cabinet Reporting

Motion:

`Move that the Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance lead creation of the cabinet evidence spine, anchored first in the enterprise synthesis, then the module-level gap/action artifacts, and finally the supporting Business Design and investigation sources.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance: accepted the duty and identified `NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement` as the narrow-scope operational anchor for source traceability.
- Director of Operations Governance: supported the motion to maintain clean handoff into recurring reporting cadence.
- Director of Portfolio and Delivery Governance: supported the motion to prevent documentation completeness from being mistaken for realized capability.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- Phase 2 draft proposal will use the evidence order established in this motion.

## Chair Summary

The chair determined that no tie-breaking action was required. The cabinet completed Phase 1 with three carried motions, one controlling evidentiary standard, four priority subject groups, and an agreed evidence-spine structure for the next phase.
