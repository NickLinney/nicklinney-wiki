## Agenda

## Official Motions and Actions

### Item 1 - Final Control Statement Adoption

Motion:

`Move that the final report open with an explicit control statement that the realization re-baseline governs all report language, and that unsupported capability claims are excluded or explicitly marked In Progress / Not Realized.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Operations Governance: supported the motion because it keeps the report from drifting into design-based overstatement.
- Director of Security Governance and Assurance: supported the motion because it prevents security-adjacent governance artifacts from being mistaken for realized security operation.
- Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance: supported the motion because it creates a reviewable provenance rule for every later claim.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- The Recording Secretary will place the control statement near the beginning of the Executive Chair report and apply it throughout the artifact.

### Item 2 - Explicit Module Set Naming and Caution Rules

Motion:

`Move that the final report explicitly name the architecture-critical dependency set as NickLinney.System, SyntheOS, NickLinney.Modules, and NickLinney.DataInfrastructure; explicitly name NickLinney.Security, NickLinney.SecOps, and NickLinney.YubiKey as In Progress / Not Realized; and preserve cautionary wording around dependency, registry, hosting, documentation, and adjacent-module realization claims.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Enterprise Architecture and Technical Governance: supported the motion because the architecture-critical set is strategically meaningful and cannot be allowed to hide inside generalized enterprise language.
- Director of Security Governance and Assurance: supported the motion because the security set must be labeled precisely to avoid false readiness impressions.
- Director of Knowledge Management and Documentation Governance: supported the motion because registry and documentation claims remain weaker than the design corpus and need explicit caution.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- The Executive Chair report will include these named module sets and their caution posture in the body of the findings.

### Item 3 - Final Submission Condition

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet approve submission of the Enterprise Capability Report to the Executive Chair only after the Recording Secretary preserves the adopted evidence order, distinguishes NickLinney.Ops, NickLinney.env, and NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement as narrow-scope footholds only, and frames delivery-governance recommendations as evidence discipline, ownership clarity, recurring proof packs, governance cadence, and realization-constrained sequencing rather than broader transformation claims.`

Comment actions:

- Director of Portfolio and Delivery Governance: supported the motion because it keeps delivery recommendations bounded to the investigated evidence.
- Director of Operations Governance: supported the motion because it preserves honest separation between the narrow operating base and the non-realized remainder.
- Director of Enterprise Architecture and Technical Governance: supported the motion because it keeps sequencing logic tied to realization proof rather than conceptual dependency alone.

Disposition:

- Carried.

Action item:

- Upon incorporation of the carried conditions, the Recording Secretary may issue the final report into the wiki as the cabinet's Phase 3 output.

## Chair Summary

No tie-breaking action was required. The chair determined that the cabinet had approved the report-control statement, the named module-set and caution rules, and the final submission condition without expanding scope beyond the investigation.
