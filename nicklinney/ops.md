# NickLinney.Ops

## Status

- Status: Canonical
- Canonicality: Canonical
- Created: July 28, 2026
- Basis: direct framework publication and prior recovered operations doctrine

## Purpose

`NickLinney.Ops` governs day-to-day operational administration of the NickLinney.* ecosystem.

It defines how capabilities are evaluated, how gaps are reported, how actions are tracked to closure, and how operational governance is converted into a durable improvement cycle.

## Scope

This module is intended to own:

- operational governance routines
- capability evaluation methods
- gap and action-planning controls
- improvement tracking and closure review
- operational reporting and review cadence
- publication-control records for Ops governance artifacts
