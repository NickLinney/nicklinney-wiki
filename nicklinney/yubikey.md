## Purpose

`NickLinney.YubiKey` governs hardware-backed identity and trust across the ecosystem.

It is expected to define how FIDO2, PIV, SSH, GPG, authorization, secrets handling, enrollment, recovery, and hardware-rooted trust controls are administered and maintained.

## Scope

This module is expected to own:

- hardware-backed identity standards
- authentication and authorization procedures
- SSH and GPG trust workflows
- enrollment and recovery procedures
- secrets-management integration guidance
- hardware-rooted trust governance
