## Working Framing

The chair opened by stating that the purpose of publication readiness is not cosmetic completeness. It is to verify that the current design package can be read by the CVO as a truthful record of what was decided, what remains open, and how the cabinet used the retrospective corpus to shape the initiative.

## Official Motions and Commentary

### Item 1 - Publication Coherence of the Design Package

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet determine the current design package to be coherent and publishable because it now contains a design landing page, ordered meeting minutes, explicit design constraints, and bounded provisional conclusions without pretending final stack selection has occurred.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that the package now reads as a real design sequence rather than as disconnected executive notes.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that the package is honest about unresolved control decisions, which is important for trustworthiness.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that the ordered meeting progression gives the initiative an auditable phase narrative suitable for executive review.
- Director of Product Management: supported the motion and added that the package now shows how system, role, approval, and secret-handling concerns affect future human and agent workflows.
- Director of Information Technology: supported the motion and noted that the recorded cautions about operational practicality materially improve the credibility of the package.
- Director of Information Security: supported the motion and stated that the package does not overstate maturity or security finality, which is the right posture.
- Vice President of Enterprise Architecture: supported the motion and added that the package now visibly preserves boundary reasoning across records, roles, and secret-control domains.
- Director of DevOps: supported the motion and clarified that machine identities and IaC pathways are no longer afterthoughts in the record.
- Documentation Manager: supported the motion and confirmed that the artifact set is now structurally suitable for wiki navigation and executive review.

Disposition:

- Carried.

### Item 2 - Traceability Sufficiency

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet determine the current package to have sufficient traceability to the CVO kickoff message and executive retrospective corpus for this phase, while recording that a later comparison artifact will be required before final technology selection can be justified.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that the CVO, CIO, CTO, and CISO inputs are clearly visible in the record and materially shaped the design decisions.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that the traceability is sufficient for phase review even though it is not yet the final architecture evidence bundle.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that the recorded deferral of final technology selection is exactly the kind of bounded incompleteness good governance should preserve.
- Director of Product Management: supported the motion and added that the package shows a clear line from enterprise need to control surfaces and future workflow impacts.
- Director of Information Technology: supported the motion and noted that the COO perspective is also visible through the repeated operational-manageability cautions.
- Director of Information Security: supported the motion and stated that the retained open comparison work is not a flaw; it is proof that the cabinet did not skip necessary evaluation.
- Vice President of Enterprise Architecture: supported the motion and added that the traceability is strong because cross-module dependency reasoning appears in more than one meeting.
- Director of DevOps: supported the motion and clarified that the CTO and CVO concerns about automation and agents are both visible in the control criteria now adopted.
- Documentation Manager: supported the motion and confirmed that the package can be navigated and cited without needing unwritten oral context.

Disposition:

- Carried.

### Item 3 - Open Items for the Next Cabinet Cycle

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet carry forward three explicit next-cycle items after CVO review: a technology comparison artifact, a draft approval matrix, and an initial implementation slice proposal, while making no claim that these have already been completed.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that the next cycle should move from design framing into comparative architecture and pilot scoping.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that the approval matrix is needed before any real trust-control rollout can be treated as design-complete.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that naming the next-cycle items now will make executive feedback easier to translate into concrete follow-on work.
- Director of Product Management: supported the motion and added that the implementation slice proposal should probably center one bounded human-plus-agent workflow rather than a broad rollout.
- Director of Information Technology: supported the motion and noted that the implementation slice must remain proportionate to current operating capacity.
- Director of Information Security: supported the motion and stated that the technology comparison artifact should explicitly preserve the distinction between human secret management and machine credential governance.
- Vice President of Enterprise Architecture: supported the motion and added that the implementation slice should be chosen with boundary clarity, not just convenience.
- Director of DevOps: supported the motion and registered a preference that the first slice include at least one service-to-service or agent-driven credential path so the CVO's vision remains grounded.
- Documentation Manager: supported the motion and confirmed that these open items can be published as next-cycle work without confusing them with completed outputs.

Disposition:

- Carried.

### Item 4 - Readiness for CVO Review

Motion:

`Move that the cabinet conclude the current design meeting corpus is ready for CVO review and executive feedback in the form of initiatives, general orders, or ad hoc strategic direction.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that the cabinet has now produced enough disciplined material for meaningful executive response.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that the cabinet should now prefer executive feedback over further speculative elaboration.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that pausing for CVO review at this point is the correct governance gate.
