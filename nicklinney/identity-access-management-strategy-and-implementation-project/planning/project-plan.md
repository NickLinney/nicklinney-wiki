## Executive Objective

Produce and then govern a bounded design-and-implementation plan for `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` that can improve enterprise coherence, access discipline, and future realization evidence without overstating present maturity.

## Planning Assumptions

- the CVO retrospective is the controlling historical input for this initiative
- the completed 2026 ECI baseline remains fixed and separate from this project
- implementation must begin with bounded slices and named control records
- meetings are working governance artifacts, not ceremonial additions
- no design-phase subordinate working meeting should commence until the CVO provides the intended kickoff input for the next phase
- the hold condition was satisfied on July 28, 2026 by formal receipt of `CVO Design Phase Kickoff Input` in the design-phase kickoff cabinet meeting

## Delivery Phases

### Initiation

- activate the CIO temporary cabinet
- receive the CVO retrospective
- produce the executive abstract

### Planning

- produce the product plan and project plan
- identify required design-phase meetings
- establish sub-meetings for control design, architecture review, and publication discipline

### Execution

- define the role catalog and module ownership map
- define the secrets access model and vault access strategy
- identify initial implementation slice and pilot boundary
- enter execution through a bounded technology comparison, approval matrix, and first-slice mobilization package authorized by the CVO on July 28, 2026

### Monitoring and Controlling

- hold recurring approval and exception reviews
- track scope, dependency, and evidence risks
- preserve decision records and update the initiative artifact register

### Closure (Maintenance)

- issue the first stable control package
- transition recurring maintenance to named operating owners
- prepare an evidence bundle for use in the next ECI cycle

## Success Criteria

- the initiative has a named temporary cabinet and approved working structure
- the initiative has an executive abstract, product plan, and project plan
- the design phase is broken into governable meetings and sub-meetings
- future implementation work can proceed from a bounded evidence-led plan
- the execution phase has a formal technical action plan, first-slice recommendation, and mobilization record
