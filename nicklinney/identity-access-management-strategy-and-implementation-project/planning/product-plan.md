## Product Intent

Design and begin implementing a governed `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` capability that supplies an enterprise-standard model for identity, authorization, privileged access, and secrets distribution across human and agent participants.

## Problem Statement

The current enterprise corpus can describe modules, governance structures, and future multi-agent architectures, but it does not yet prove a sufficiently governed identity-and-access spine. As a result, ownership, permissions, secret access, and trust boundaries remain easier to describe than to verify.

## Product Scope

### In Scope

- enterprise RBAC model
- module-to-role ownership mapping
- privileged-action classification
- secrets access model
- secure vault access strategy
- review, approval, and evidence expectations

### Out of Scope for This Initial Package

- full enterprise identity platform deployment
- blanket retrofit of every existing module
- implied realization of adjacent security or systems capabilities

## Product Capabilities

1. Role catalog for executives, directors, operational staff, and agents
2. Access policy model tied to module boundaries and systems-of-record
3. Secrets classification and vault access workflow
4. Human-agent authority delegation model
5. Reviewable audit and exception process

## Primary Stakeholders

- CVO for enterprise coherence
- CIO for systems governance
- CISO for assurance and control
- PMO Director for execution governance
- Director of Information Technology for operational application
- Director of DevOps for automation and integration pathways

## Evidence of Product Readiness

- approved role catalog
- approved control-boundary map
- documented secrets workflow
- designated systems of record for identity and access decisions
- review cadence with named approvers and records

## Product Risks

- over-scoping into generalized architecture redesign
- designing a control model that exceeds the current operational estate
- conflating documented intent with active realization

## Product Milestones

1. Executive framing and cabinet constitution
2. Design-phase meetings and artifact production
3. Control model review and approval
4. Implementation sequencing and pilot selection
5. Ongoing maintenance and reassessment preparation
