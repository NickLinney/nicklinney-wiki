## Execution Guardrails

- do not imply that adjacent security, platform, or product capabilities are fully realized by virtue of this initiative alone
- prefer one governed implementation slice over broad rollout claims
- retain explicit separation between publication records, operational identities, secret values, and runtime evidence
- keep the human and machine access models integrated, but do not collapse them into one convenience-first tooling assumption
