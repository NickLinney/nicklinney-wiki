## Executive Summary

The CVO retrospective identifies identity and access governance as the most leverage-bearing next move for the enterprise because it can improve both `NickLinney.Strategy` and `NickLinney.Modules` without pretending that wider enterprise realization has already been achieved. The initiative is therefore framed as a bounded strategy-and-implementation project rather than as a general transformation program.

## Initiative Purpose

The purpose of the initiative is to establish a practical enterprise identity-and-access operating model that can govern:

- role-based access control across approved enterprise modules and actors
- secrets access boundaries and vault-mediated distribution patterns
- human and agent authority relationships
- evidence-bearing review of privileged access decisions

## Why This Initiative Now

The ECI found that the enterprise is strongly designed but operationally under-realized. In that condition, identity and access discipline becomes a coherence multiplier. It improves the truthfulness of module ownership, the defensibility of systems-of-record claims, and the reliability of future security and automation planning.

## Initial Deliverables

## Initial Success Condition

The initiative will have succeeded at this first stage when the enterprise has a governable and reviewable design package for `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` that can be advanced without overstating current operational maturity.
