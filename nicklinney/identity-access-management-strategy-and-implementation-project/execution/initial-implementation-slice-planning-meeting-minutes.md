## Official Motions and Commentary

### Item 1 - First-Slice Boundary

Motion:

`Move that the first execution slice be bounded to the IAM initiative's own implementation and documentation pathway, proving one human request path and one machine credential path before any broader rollout is attempted.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that self-referential governance is the fastest honest path to real evidence.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that a narrow slice reduces trust risk while still testing the key control model.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that the slice is appropriately bounded and maps directly to the project plan's call for a pilot boundary.
- Director of Product Management: supported the motion and added that the slice still exercises the essential human and machine journeys the CVO called for.
- Director of Information Technology: supported the motion and noted that the boundary is practical for current operations.
- Director of Information Security: supported the motion and stressed that the machine path must include revocation and review, not only issuance.
- Vice President of Enterprise Architecture: supported the motion and added that the chosen slice is architecturally meaningful because it crosses governance, platform, and automation surfaces.
- Director of DevOps: supported the motion and confirmed that the machine path should represent a real service or agent workflow rather than a synthetic demonstration.
- Documentation Manager: supported the motion and stated that the slice can be fully traced and published without unnecessary disclosure.

Disposition:

- Carried.
