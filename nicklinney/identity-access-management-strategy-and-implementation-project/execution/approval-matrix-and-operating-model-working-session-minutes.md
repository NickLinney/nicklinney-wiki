## Official Motions and Commentary

### Item 1 - Adoption of the Draft Approval Matrix for First-Slice Use

Motion:

`Move that the draft approval matrix be adopted for first-slice execution use, subject to later refinement after pilot evidence is collected.`

Commentary:

- Chief Information Officer: supported the motion and stated that the matrix is now concrete enough to govern an actual pilot.
- Chief Information Security Officer: supported the motion and clarified that the matrix properly preserves separate approval surfaces for module, security, and platform authority.
- PMO Director: supported the motion and stated that execution now has a usable decision instrument rather than an abstract governance note.
- Director of Product Management: supported the motion and added that the matrix maps cleanly to the pilot's request and approval journey.
- Director of Information Technology: supported the motion and reiterated the need to record any duty consolidation explicitly.
- Director of Information Security: supported the motion and stated that the treatment of emergency access is appropriately strict for the current maturity level.
- Vice President of Enterprise Architecture: supported the motion and noted that the matrix is aligned with the federated systems-of-record approach.
- Director of DevOps: supported the motion and clarified that machine credential approvals are now visible and reviewable rather than implied.
- Documentation Manager: supported the motion and confirmed that the matrix is publication-ready.

Disposition:

- Carried.
