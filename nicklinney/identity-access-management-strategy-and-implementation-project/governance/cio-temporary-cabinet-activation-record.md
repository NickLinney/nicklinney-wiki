## Activation Basis

This temporary cabinet is activated by the CIO after receipt of the historical CVO retrospective. Its purpose is to produce the initial executive abstract, product plan, and project plan for the CVO's new directive: establish the `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` Strategy and Implementation Project as a standalone initiative.

## Scope

- receive and interpret the CVO retrospective as a bounded design mandate
- form a temporary cabinet from approved company positions only
- produce the initial executive abstract, product plan, and project plan
- conduct the design-phase meeting sequence needed to structure the initiative

## Governing Constraints

1. The completed 2026 ECI baseline is not to be revised by this initiative package.
2. `NickLinney.IdentityAccessManagement` remains a forward-looking initiative and not part of the completed assessed module set.
3. Positions and agent assignments must use the approved canonical roster.
4. The cabinet should prefer bounded governance and implementation planning over broad enterprise redesign.
