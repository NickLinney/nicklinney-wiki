## Initiative Position

This initiative is a meaningful secondary follow-on program informed by the corrected CVO retrospective. It is not the CVO's top action item for the current year-one priority stack, and it is separate from the standalone IAM initiative.

## Problem Statement

`NickLinney.BusinessDesign` contains a substantial body of enterprise-shaping patterns and governance logic, but the ECI evidence did not prove it as a recurring operating control capability. The enterprise therefore risks relying on strong design language without equivalent runtime operating standards.

## Bounded Objective

Convert the highest-value business-design patterns into controlled operating standards, governance rules, and recurring review artifacts with named ownership and evidence-bearing use in live enterprise work.

## Expected Evidence of Progress

- approved operating standards derived from business-design patterns
- named ownership and review obligations
- recurring governance records showing use of those standards
- traceable adoption of those standards in active enterprise work

## Dependency Notes

The initiative depends on `NickLinney.Ops` for cadence, `NickLinney.KnowledgeManagement` for durable publication, and the CVO's ongoing governance of enterprise design and strategy.
