## Executive Summary

The CMO retrospective shows that communications capability remains largely conceptual, but also bounded enough to be proven through one controlled internal workflow. This initiative therefore creates a pilot program focused on establishing one governed communications pathway with explicit channel rules, review discipline, and retained evidence rather than implying broad communications maturity.

## Initiative Purpose

The purpose of the initiative is to prove one internal communications capability through:

- a documented operating model and scope for the selected workflow
- explicit channel, security, and service expectations
- retained records for communications review, action, and closure
- operational ownership for the governed pilot path

## Why This Initiative Now

The closed ECI corpus found no direct evidence of an active communications deployment under `NickLinney.Comms`. Because the module is bounded, a pilot can establish a real foothold without heavy structural expansion. This makes the initiative an efficient way to move from concept to governed use while staying honest about current maturity.

## Initial Deliverables

- executive abstract
- communications workflow scope note and operating model
- controlled service requirements for the selected internal channel
- review, retention, and closure evidence standards

## Initial Success Condition

The initiative will have succeeded at this first stage when one internal communications workflow can be reviewed as a governed operating capability with explicit ownership, channel rules, and retained evidence.
