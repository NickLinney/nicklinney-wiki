# Safety and Assurance Charter

## Status

- Classification: Constitutional Governance
- Applies to: all NickLinney.* products, projects, services, infrastructure, agents, models, and operational environments

## Executive Statement

NickLinney.Security exists to ensure that every component within the NickLinney.* ecosystem is designed, implemented, operated, and evolved according to a unified philosophy of safety, security, governance, and accountability.

The ecosystem is regarded not merely as software, but as an evolving cybernetic infrastructure whose growing autonomy requires the rigor associated with safety-critical and high-assurance systems.

## Constitutional Principles

### Principle 1: Maximum Risk

Systems are evaluated according to the greatest credible harm they could enable, not merely their intended purpose.

### Principle 2: Safety Before Capability

No feature is complete until its safety properties are designed, implemented, tested, documented, and accepted.

### Principle 3: Governance Before Automation

Automation operates only within explicitly defined authority boundaries.

### Principle 4: Human Accountability

Ultimate responsibility remains with human operators.

### Principle 5: Explainability

Consequential decisions should be reconstructable through provenance, evidence, reasoning, policy references, and execution history.

### Principle 6: Reversibility

Where practical, changes should support rollback, reproducibility, recovery, audit, and post-incident analysis.

### Principle 7: Least Authority

Every process, service, model, workflow, agent, and operator should receive only the minimum authority required.

## Scope

This charter governs:

- software systems
- source control and CI/CD
- infrastructure and operations
- data platforms
- AI orchestration
- autonomous agents
- research environments
- third-party integrations

No project exists outside this governance framework.

## Assurance Domains

The source charter defines assurance expectations across at least these domains:

- architectural assurance
- software assurance
- operational assurance
- AI assurance
