# Knowledge Management Plan

## Purpose

This plan operationalizes the Knowledge Management Strategy for the specific goal of populating and maintaining NickLinney.Wiki with full versioning and historical retention.

## Phase 1: Establish Control Framework

Create and adopt the core control artifacts:

- Document Control and Publication Standard
- Document Naming and Ownership Standard
- Enterprise Document Register Schema
- Chat-Generated Artifact Recovery Procedure
- Canonical Source Declaration Record Standard

Output: the wiki gains the rules required to classify and govern documentation before large-scale expansion.

## Phase 2: Register the Known Estate

Take the existing enterprise inventory and convert logical entries into a managed register.

Required actions:

- assign document classes
- assign owning modules
- capture known status
- capture current source location where known
- mark confidence and recovery priority
- note canonical-source ambiguity

Output: the knowledge estate becomes sortable and triageable.

## Phase 3: Recover High-Value Canonical Artifacts

Prioritize recovery of documents that are foundational, frequently referenced, or structurally upstream.

Initial candidates include:

- Risk Classification Framework
- Portfolio and Module Registry
- Repository Information Architecture Standard
- Architectural Decision Record Standard
- Virtual Cybernetic Enterprise SOP
- module executive abstracts with high cross-reference value

Output: the most important conceptual bottlenecks stop living only in conversation history.

## Phase 4: Module-by-Module Population

For each module, create:

- module landing page
- core governing standards
n- key records and templates
- relationships to products, projects, and evidence

Priority order should begin with governance-heavy and cross-cutting modules.

## Phase 5: Historical Retention and Supersession

Define and apply handling rules for:

- superseded pages
- deprecated standards
- archived projects
- replaced templates
- recovered-but-not-yet-approved artifacts

Output: the wiki becomes a historical record, not merely a current-state brochure.

## Phase 6: Ongoing Intake Procedure

All new documents should enter through a controlled intake path that captures:

- title and class
- owning module
- status
- origin
- approval authority
- version
- relationships
- retention expectations

## Operating Cadence

Recommended recurring cycle:

- weekly recovery and publication review
- monthly register reconciliation
- quarterly supersession and archival audit
- event-driven canonical-source resolution when duplicates are discovered

## Measures of Completion

The plan is materially complete when:

- the register covers the known inventory
- every foundational artifact has either a canonical page or explicit recovery status
- lifecycle states are consistently visible
- the wiki can explain both the current truth and the history of how it got there
