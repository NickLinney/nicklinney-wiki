# Knowledge Management Strategy

## Strategic Objective

Build NickLinney.Wiki into the authoritative, historically durable, provenance-aware knowledge space for the NickLinney.* ecosystem.

## Target Outcome

Every important enterprise artifact should become traceable across five dimensions:

- identity
- authority
- version
- provenance
- relationship

## Strategic Principles

### Documentation Is a First-Class Asset

Documentation is governed with the same seriousness as code, infrastructure, and operational state.

### Canonicality Must Be Explicit

A page is not authoritative merely because it exists. Canonical source, owner, approval state, and supersession relationship must be declared.

### Recovery Before Reinvention

When a document already exists in chat, uploads, or composite fragments, the first preference is controlled recovery and normalization rather than unnecessary regeneration.

### Versioning Must Preserve History

The wiki should not erase prior states conceptually. Superseded knowledge remains historically meaningful and should stay discoverable through retained records, revision history, and status declarations.

### Provenance Must Travel With Content

Every controlled artifact should retain its origin story, such as uploaded source, recovered chat markdown, composite synthesis, or derived standard.

### Separation of Document Classes Is Mandatory

Governance, strategy, standards, templates, products, projects, operations, evidence, and external references must remain distinguishable.

## Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: Controlled Population

Populate the wiki in a deliberate order:

1. constitutional and governance artifacts
2. control standards and schemas
3. module-level executive and operating pages
4. product and project records
5. supporting templates and references

### Pillar 2: Enterprise Register Backbone

Use a document register as the authoritative inventory layer backing the wiki. The register should track each logical artifact even when the artifact is not yet fully recovered.

### Pillar 3: Historical Retention by Status

Retain historical meaning through explicit lifecycle states such as draft, active, superseded, deprecated, archived, and evidence-only.

### Pillar 4: Recovery of Fragmented Artifacts

Run a systematic recovery program for chat-only, bundle-embedded, and section-only documents.

### Pillar 5: Cross-System Durability

Where feasible, align wiki pages with repository-backed or exportable source artifacts so that the knowledge estate survives tool migration.

## Strategy Success Criteria

The strategy is succeeding when:

- every tracked artifact has a register entry
- high-value artifacts have a canonical page or controlled source record
- duplicate copies are reconciled through canonical-source declarations
- superseded material remains historically intelligible
- future artifacts are born into the control system instead of recovered later
